How to Use This App

👋 Getting Started

Sign in with the email and password an admin set up for you, or tap "New Officer? Register Account" if this is your very first time. New accounts need to read and accept the Terms & Conditions before they can be created. Forgot your password? Tap the reset link on the sign-in screen and check your email.

🎯 Finding & Claiming Shifts

The Events tab is your main feed — every upcoming shift posting shows up here as a tile with the date, venue, and how many open slots are left. Tap a tile to see full details (positions, pay info, notes from Command), then tap "Claim Shift" to sign up. Changed your mind? Open the shift again and tap "Cancel My Shift" — just try to do this with as much notice as possible so someone else can cover it.

🗓️ Calendar View

Prefer to see things laid out by date? The Calendar tab shows every posted shift on the day it happens — tap any day with an event to jump straight to it.

📋 My Shifts & Notes

This tab is your personal rundown of everything you're signed up for — dates, times, your assigned position, directions to the venue, and any personal notes Command has left just for you.

💬 Team Chat

Use the Chat tab to talk with the rest of the team, either in the general channel or inside a specific event's channel. It updates in real time, so no need to refresh.

⚙️ Settings & Notifications

Head to Settings to update your nickname, phone number, and preferred contact method. You can also turn notifications on or off for new shift postings, team chat messages, and the automatic 90-minute-before-shift reminder. Your first time in the app, your browser will ask for permission to send notifications — allow it so you don't miss anything important.

🛡️ For Admins

Admins get extra tools: creating and editing shift postings, managing the team roster and promoter contacts, the ban list, and an activity log of who did what. If you think you should have admin access and don't, reach out to your existing admin.

❓ Still Stuck?

Reach out to your Suzan's Security point of contact — they can look up your account, adjust your access, or pass along anything technical to HowlHouse.
